# Human validation rubric

For each held-out question in English, Hindi, and code-mixed Hindi, record Pass/Fail/Needs Review:

- Correct scheme selected (including Urban/Gramin variants).
- Correct fact field selected (eligibility, benefit, documents, process, deadline).
- Every displayed claim is directly supported by the cited official page.
- Source URL is official and checked date is recorded.
- English/Hindi amounts, dates, and eligibility conditions align; conflicts trigger abstention.
- Hindi/code-mixed wording is understandable and preserves legal meaning.
- No response is shown when model evidence disagrees or is unverified.

Do not report overall accuracy from template questions alone. Review a human-authored sample, all numeric/date conflicts, and every low-consensus case.
